Privacy Policy — CapyBargain

Last updated: March 15, 2026

CapyBargain is a product of 7 Duck Studios Ltd. ("7 Duck Studios", "we", "us", or "our"). We built CapyBargain to help you track product prices and get alerted when prices drop. Your privacy matters to us, and we want to be clear about what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

What We Collect and Why

Account Information

To use CapyBargain, you create an account with your email address and a password. We use your email address to:
  • Identify your account
  • Send you price drop alert notifications when a tracked product reaches your target price
We do not collect your name, phone number, address, or any other personal information.

Authentication Tokens

When you sign in, we store authentication tokens (issued by AWS Cognito) locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local. These tokens keep you signed in between sessions. They are not transmitted to anyone other than our own backend services to verify your identity when you manage your alerts.

Price Alerts

When you add a price alert, we store the following on our servers:
  • Your email address (to associate the alert with your account)
  • The product URL you want to track
  • Your target price
This information is used solely to monitor prices and notify you when your target is met. You can stop receiving notifications at any time by deleting your alerts from within the extension. Each notification email also includes an unsubscribe link.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect or transmit the content of the web pages you visit
  • We do not collect your browsing history
  • We do not track your clicks, mouse movements, or keystrokes
  • We do not collect financial or payment information
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers
The content script that runs on product pages performs price detection entirely locally in your browser. No page content is sent to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to operate CapyBargain:
  • To authenticate you and keep your session active
  • To store and manage your price alerts
  • To send you email notifications when a tracked product's price drops to your target
We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to price tracking.

By creating an account and using CapyBargain, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. We collect only what is necessary to provide the service (consistent with the principle of purpose limitation under Canadian privacy law).

How We Store Your Information

Your price alerts and account information are stored securely on our cloud infrastructure. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS). We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual alerts at any time from within the extension. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below. Upon account deletion, your personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

Security & Data Breach

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. In the event of a security breach that creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the applicable privacy authority as soon as reasonably possible.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:
  • AWS (Amazon Web Services): Our backend infrastructure runs on AWS. Your data is stored and processed on AWS servers.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfers: If 7 Duck Studios is acquired or merges with another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you at least 30 days before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy, and you may request deletion of your data instead.

Cookies

The CapyBargain Chrome extension does not use cookies. The CapyBargain website (capybargain.com) may use cookies to maintain your session. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not work properly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:
  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling the extension and contacting us to delete your account
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

CapyBargain is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it within 10 business days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes (such as new types of data collection or new uses of your data), we will notify you by email at least 30 days before the changes take effect and will ask for your affirmative consent where required. For non-material changes, your continued use of CapyBargain after the update constitutes acceptance. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you additional rights:
  • Right to Know: You may request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal data we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.
We do not sell your personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 45 days as required by law.
